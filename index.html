A LEGACY IN MOTION

A century of engineering.

BMW was founded in 1916 in Munich, Germany. Over time, the company became known for combining performance, comfort and driver engagement.

Today, the lineup includes sedans, SUVs and electric vehicles. Each model offers a different interpretation of the driving experience.
